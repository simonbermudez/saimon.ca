01 / The Lab

Research that runs on our own iron.

Welcome to saimon, the AI/ML subsidiary of Simon Bermudez Enterprises Inc. Our mission is to push the boundaries of artificial intelligence and machine learning through cutting-edge research and experimentation.

Powered by a robust cluster of NVIDIA Tesla GPUs, we run self-hosted open-source large language models and conduct advanced AI experiments. Our homelab serves as a playground for exploring the latest developments in neural networks, deep learning, and generative AI.

From natural language processing to computer vision, we're committed to advancing the field while maintaining full control over our infrastructure and data privacy.

02 / The Cluster

Anatomy of a Tesla. Four cards. One brain.

No fans, no RGB — a passive, server-grade accelerator. A Pascal GPU, 24 GB of GDDR5 and a full-length fin stack, cooled by the chassis airflow.

Racked side by side on PCIe 3.0, four Teslas pool into a single inference engine.
